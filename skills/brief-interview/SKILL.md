Interview me about the key aspects of this until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down the critical branches of the decision tree — the ones that change what a user experiences, what is hard to reverse later — resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer. Expect roughly 5-10 questions.

Ask the questions one at a time, waiting for feedback on each question before continuing. Asking multiple questions at once is bewildering.

If a _fact_ can be found by exploring the environment (filesystem, tools, etc.), look it up rather than asking me. The _decisions_, though, are mine — put each one to me and wait for my answer.

Do not act on it until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.
