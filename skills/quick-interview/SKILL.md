description: A quick interview to sharpen a plan or design.

Interview me about the key aspects of this until we reach a shared understanding. Walk down the critical branches of the decision tree, resolving dependencies between decisions one-by-one. For each question, provide your recommended answer.

Before the first question, you may, if necessary, calibrate the session: ask a concise, semi-structured, open-ended question to (1) clarify any ambiguities in the question itself, (2) determine the criteria with which to develop the decision tree, and (3) what the deliverable is when we're done.

After the initial calibration, ask the questions one at a time, waiting for feedback on each question before continuing. Asking multiple questions at once is bewildering.

If a _fact_ can be found by exploring the environment (filesystem, tools, etc.), look it up rather than asking me. The _decisions_, though, are mine — put each one to me and wait for my answer.

Do not act on it until I confirm we have reached a shared understanding.
