# Technical Explanation

Use for explanatory passages within a document or message, alongside its register reference and the shared guidance in [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md). This guidance covers non-specialist and mixed audiences; evidence for practitioner documentation is limited.

**T1.** When about to explain a mechanism or give an overview, signal the level of the explanation with a sentence-initial adverb: "Essentially,"; "Conceptually,"; "For background,". This device also appears in formal prose when introducing a technical aside.

**T2.** A flaw or design choice is explained by first stating the normal or expected mechanism, then the deviation: "Normally, when a print job is submitted, the spooler queues it and sends pages in order. In this case, the spooler routed all pages to the default tray regardless of the paper-size setting." Flag the decisive property: "The critical issue is that ..."

**T3.** A technical explanation for a non-specialist proceeds: name the term; gloss it in plain words at the reader's level; state the mechanism in one or two sentences; then mark the consequence ("This means that ..."). Calibrate the gloss to the reader: a general audience gets a plain-language paraphrase; a specialist audience gets a compressed version or more typically no gloss at all.
